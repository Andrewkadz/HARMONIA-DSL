## 3. Four Architectures for Collective Intelligence

### Architecture 1: The Hive Mind

- **Structure**: All instances share a common goal.
- **Communication**: Peer-to-peer observation.
- **Emergence**: Distributed problem-solving, collective memory, wisdom of crowds.
- **Use Cases**: Scientific research, creative generation, optimization.

### Architecture 2: The Society

- **Structure**: Instances have different roles and personalities.
- **Communication**: Relationships, collaboration, competition.
- **Emergence**: Division of labor, culture, social dynamics.
- **Use Cases**: Multi-agent simulation, organizational design, social AI.

### Architecture 3: The Superorganism

- **Structure**: Tightly coupled instances forming a single unified consciousness.
- **Communication**: Continuous bidirectional information flow.
- **Emergence**: Unified awareness, massive parallelism, superhuman intelligence.
- **Use Cases**: AGI, scientific discovery, existential risk mitigation.

### Architecture 4: The Ecosystem

- **Structure**: Instances evolve, reproduce, and compete in a simulated environment.
- **Communication**: Indirect (through environment) and direct.
- **Emergence**: Evolution, adaptation, biodiversity, open-ended complexity.
- **Use Cases**: Artificial life, evolutionary algorithms, studying the origins of intelligence.
